Kingdom Local

Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

The short version: we only collect what's needed to list your business, we only contact you about your listing, and we never send spam or share or sell your details.

Who we are

Kingdom Local is a free, community directory of Christian-owned businesses, run by James Norman through PocketRocket Solutions on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland.

What we collect

When you add your business, we collect the details you type into the form:

  • Your business name, what you do, category, suburb and the areas you serve.
  • Any contact details you choose to add: website, phone, email, social media and Google Business Profile link.
  • Your own name, so we know who to speak to about the listing.

If you're just browsing or searching the directory, we don't ask for anything. Searching happens in your own browser, and we don't use cookies, analytics or tracking.

What's shown publicly

Once a listing is approved, everything you submit is shown on the site except your own name. Anyone can see public listings, so only add contact details you're happy for people to use.

How we use your details

  • To check and publish your listing.
  • To contact you about your listing if we need to, for example to confirm a detail or let you know about a change to the directory that affects you.

That's all. We won't add you to a mailing list, send you marketing, or pass your details on to anyone else.

Where your details are kept

Listings are stored in a private Google Sheet that only we can access. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. Like most web services, these providers may record basic technical information such as your IP address when the page loads. We don't receive or use that information.

Changing or removing your listing

You can ask us to update or remove your listing at any time. Email james@pocketrocketsolutions.com.au and we'll take care of it, usually within a few days. When a listing is removed, we delete your details from our sheet as well.

Questions

If you have any questions about your details or this policy, email james@pocketrocketsolutions.com.au. If we make changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
