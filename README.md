# Kingdom Local

**Find and support Christian-owned businesses in your community.**

Kingdom Local started on the Sunshine Coast, when a simple Facebook post asking people to share Christian businesses received hundreds of replies in one night. It was a beautiful picture of a community wanting to back one another. But a comment thread isn't a directory, and within a week those businesses would be buried and impossible to find.

So we built a home for them.

## Why it exists

> "Let your light shine before others, that they may see your good deeds and glorify your Father in heaven." Matthew 5:16

Every day, believers run cafes, fix roofs, counsel families, teach kids, cut hair and build homes. Their work is part of their calling. Kingdom Local makes it easy for the community to find them, choose them, and keep money and opportunity flowing between people who share the same values.

## How it works

- **Free, always.** Listing a business costs nothing, and searching costs nothing.
- **Opt-in only.** Businesses add themselves. Nobody is listed without saying yes.
- **Checked by a real person.** Every listing is reviewed before it goes live.
- **Searchable.** Search by business name, what they do, or suburb, and filter by category or area.

## Sponsorships support missions

Kingdom Local will never charge businesses to be listed. When businesses choose to sponsor a region, their gift goes directly to a Christian mission partner, never through us. The directory becomes a way for local businesses to give together, and every sponsorship is shown publicly so the community can see the impact.

## Where we are

Starting with the **Sunshine Coast**, Queensland. More regions will follow as communities ask for them.

## Get listed

Visit the site and tap **"Add your business, it's free"**.

---

### For maintainers

- `index.html` is the whole site: search, filters and the signup form. Site name and settings are in the `CONFIG` block near the bottom.
- Listings live in a Google Sheet. A small Google Apps Script receives signups and serves approved listings to the site.
- To approve a listing, tick **Approved** in the sheet. It appears on the site within about 2 minutes.
- Hosted free on GitHub Pages.
